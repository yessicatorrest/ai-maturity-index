# AI Maturity Index

Interactive companion to my MSc thesis at TUM School of Management, in
collaboration with the BCG x TUM research cluster.

**Live app:** https://yessicatorrest.github.io/ai-maturity-index/

The index scores firms on AI maturity using only public evidence: dictionary
based NLP on annual report disclosures, AI patent filings, AI leadership
appointments and AI related acquisitions. Financial performance measures are
excluded by design, so the score stays independent of company results.

- 1,539 firms, fiscal years 2015 to 2024
- 12 features across 7 dimensions and 3 capability layers
- Validated against the HG Insights AI 1000 and the IMD AI Maturity ranking

This app shows eight firms selected.
