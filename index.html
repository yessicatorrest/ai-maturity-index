From Signals to Scores

A transparent, disclosure-based measure of corporate AI maturity, benchmarked against two commercial indexes.
Dictionary-based NLP on corporate disclosures/AI patent records (PATSTAT)/Executive and board databases/AI-related M&A activity
Most AI maturity rankings are proprietary. You cannot see what they measure or reproduce how they scored a company. This index is built entirely from public evidence: what firms disclose in annual reports, the AI patents they file, the AI leadership roles they create, and the AI companies they acquire. What predicts AI maturity

Two of three hypotheses were not supported. That is a result, not a gap. It narrows where the real explanation for AI capability differences is likely to sit.

Spearman correlations across 1,539 firms, all p < .001. Moderate agreement is the goal. A near perfect correlation would mean the index simply restates what the paid benchmarks already say.

When two paid benchmarks place the same firm five ranks apart, neither can be treated as the answer on its own. That gap is what a transparent, reproducible third measure is for.

What the index measures

Seven dimensions across three layers, each built from public evidence. Open to see what each one covers.ShowHide▾

Every dimension is equally weighted, and no financial performance measure enters the index. That keeps the score independent of company results, so the relationship between AI maturity and performance can be tested rather than assumed.
